# Citation audit — 11 September 2026

The counterexample is pinned to Nestoridi and Yan, arXiv:2409.16387v1,
Conjecture 1.6. The official 2025 FPSAC extended abstract was inspected: its
Theorem 1.2 uses an `N^2` denominator, it treats the unequal-class model, and
it explicitly omits the limit profile while directing readers to the longer
preprint. The official FPSAC poster was separately inspected and restates the
limit-profile conjecture. Neither later item changes which exact statement is
contradicted.

The official *Annals of Applied Probability* future-papers page listed the
Nestoridi–Yan article as accepted on 11 September 2026. No public final journal
text was located in the bounded search. This release therefore does not claim
that the accepted version retains or withdraws the conjecture; it identifies
the contradicted arXiv-v1 statement exactly.

Teyssier's uniform-transposition result was checked in the published 2020
*Annals of Probability* article, DOI 10.1214/20-AOP1424, with the time
normalisation cross-checked against arXiv:1905.08514v1. Recent hitting-time and
separation results cited in the manuscript were treated as related scope, not
as proof inputs for the new counterexample. This was a targeted audit through
11 September 2026, not exhaustive priority clearance.
