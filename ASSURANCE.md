# Assurance boundary

The written proofs in `paper.md`, `paper.tex` and `paper.pdf` are the evidence
for the asymptotic theorems. `certificate_contract.json` binds the exact
checker to the published profile constants, rational margins, finite table
rows and saved-trajectory dimensions. The producer replay checks rational
inequalities, four finite event bounds, eight finite-state trajectories and a
small dense spectral comparison. Hostile regression tests demonstrate
rejection of 23 specified mutations. The claim-surface checker binds five
headline equations and Table 1 to the exact contract. Its `--documents` mode
compares generated TeX and PDF text with a fresh Markdown build.

Those facts do **not** turn the asymptotic argument into a formal proof, close
the semantic bridge between prose and code automatically, establish novelty
or priority, or constitute independent external reproduction or peer review.
The five-role editorial review is model-assisted internal scrutiny of frozen
artifacts. Source inspection and CI establish only the facts they explicitly
record.
