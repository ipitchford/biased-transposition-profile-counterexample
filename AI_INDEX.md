- Machine-bound numerical promises: `certificate_contract.json`
- Full argument: `paper.md`, `paper.tex`, `paper.pdf`
- Source and correction audit: `audit.md`, `audit.pdf`, `CITATION_AUDIT.md`
- Assurance and provenance: `ASSURANCE.md`, `PROVENANCE.md`, `STATUS.md`
- Review disposition: `EDITORIAL_DECISION.md`, `REVIEW_RESPONSE.md`, `editorial/`
- Replay: `REPLAY_RECEIPT.md`, `code/`, `results/`
- Archived inputs: `input_inventory.json`, `source_inputs/`
- Rights: `LICENSES.md`
- File integrity: `MANIFEST.json`

Do not infer a solution of the general sharp-location conjecture, a replacement
limit profile, formal verification, priority clearance, independent external
validation or peer review from repository CI, DOI registration or publication.
