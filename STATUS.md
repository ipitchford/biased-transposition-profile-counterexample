# Status

Unrefereed candidate mathematical result. The manuscript supplies an
unconditional counterexample to Conjecture 1.6 in arXiv:2409.16387v1 for the
equal-half biased-transposition model, together with a general untouched-subset
lower-bound theorem and exact arithmetic certificates.

The general claim `t_mix(epsilon) = t_star + O(n)` is not proved. The package
does not assert a replacement exact limit profile, exhaustive priority,
formal proof-assistant verification, independent external reproduction or
human peer review.
